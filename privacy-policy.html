Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Giftpose collects when you use our platform, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

1 Overview

Giftpose ("we", "our", or "us") operates a community gifting platform connecting people who have items they no longer need with people who could use them. This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile application, website, and all associated services (collectively, the "Platform").

By using the Giftpose Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Platform.

We are committed to handling your personal data responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) where applicable.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated automatically when you use the Platform, and information from third-party sources where permitted.

Account Information

Name, email address, profile photo, and login credentials when you register.

Gift Listings

Photos, descriptions, item categories, and location data attached to gifts you post.

Location Data

Approximate or precise location to connect you with nearby donors and recipients.

Device & Usage

IP address, device type, OS, app version, pages visited, and interactions with the Platform.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal data, please contact us immediately.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and improve the Platform. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and manage your Giftpose account and profile.
  • Facilitate connections between donors and recipients.
  • Send you gift alerts, notifications, and platform updates.
  • Personalise your feed and recommend relevant gifts near you.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or policy violations.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve product features and user experience.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we will never use your data to display third-party advertisements without your explicit consent.

4 Sharing Your Information

We share your information only in the limited circumstances described below:

Other Users: Your display name, profile photo, and listing details are visible to other users as part of the gifting experience.

Service Providers: Trusted vendors (hosting, analytics, customer support) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality obligations.

Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

5 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time via in-app settings or by contacting us.

Certain data may be retained for longer periods where we have a legitimate legal or regulatory basis — such as transaction records, fraud prevention logs, or where required by applicable law.

Anonymised and aggregated data (which cannot identify you) may be retained indefinitely for analytical and product improvement purposes.

6 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Restriction: Request that we limit processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@giftpose.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7 Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse traffic, and remember your preferences.

Essential Cookies: Required for the Platform to function correctly. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the Platform. Data is aggregated and anonymised.

Preference Cookies: Store your settings such as language and region preferences.

You can manage or disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain features of the Platform.
